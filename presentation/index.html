Proprietary Run-Sheet Engine

Synchronizing 15+ Teams in 15-Minute Windows

Our master timeline orchestrates priests, live vocalists, decorators, and caterers so no ritual is rushed, no guest waits for food, and the sacred auspicious muhurat is met with total serenity.

  • Pre-Event T-120: Hall handover, sound-check & flower petal path setup.
  • T-00 to T+60: Welcome with ittar & gajra; live Sugam Sangeet starts.
  • T+90 to T+150: Sacred muhurat, custom Antarpat unveil, flower shower.
  • T+150: Wave-based dining transition with Khan-bordered service.
