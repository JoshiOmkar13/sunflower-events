Upanayan (Bratabandha) & Traditional Milestones

मुंज / व्रतबंध — संस्कार आणि मांगल्याचे क्षण

🪔 100% Vedic Sanctity • विधींचे पावित्र्य व साहित्याची अचूकता
6 Sacred Ritual Milestones • षोडश संस्कार टप्पे
1. Matribhojan (मातृभोजन)
Special ceremonial course served on silver / banana plates with maternal blessings.
2. Keshavapan (केशवपण)
Ritual tonsure handled with calm care, hygiene, and soothing assistance for the child.
3. Illustrated Antarpat (अंतरपाट)
Bespoke 6-panel cloth with Gayatri mantra, held precisely at the sacred muhurt.
4. Gayatri Upadesh (गायत्री उपदेश)
Sacred whispering of the Gayatri mantra under the ceremonial silk shawl.
5. Samidha Daan (समिधा दान)
Havan offerings executed with safe fire buffer and authentic Vedic samagri.
6. Bhikshaval Procession (भिक्षावळ)
Floral petal carpet walkway and traditional brass umbrella for the child.
