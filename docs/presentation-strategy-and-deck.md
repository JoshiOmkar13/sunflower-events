## 4. Typography Hierarchy Recommendation

To ensure seamless bilingual harmony between English and Marathi (Devanagari):

- **English Display Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Bold, 800) paired with `Playfair Display` or `Cinzel` for editorial authority.
- **Marathi Headings:** `Noto Serif Devanagari` (SemiBold / Bold) or `Rozha One` — classical, regal, and effortlessly readable.
- **Body Text & Metadata (Both):** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400, 500, 600) + `Noto Sans Devanagari` for crisp legibility across high-DPI retina displays and mobile phones.
